System Quest Board

⚔ Working Hours & Daily Schedule

  • Standard lab hours as set by the coordinator/admin roster.
  • Presence is logged daily and feeds the weekly presence streak.

⚔ Reporting & Attendance

  • Daily task reporting is required to maintain the task reporting streak.
  • 3 flex days per month are tracked and deducted from late-arrival counts before penalising.

⚔ Task Management

  • Tasks are assigned by U5 Coordinators or Admin with duration, deadline, and priority.
  • Members mark tasks "Complete"; the assigning coordinator then rates the work.
  • Points are only awarded after coordinator rating — never on self-completion.

⚔ Point Earning Criteria

  • Start at 100 pts on member creation.
  • Task rated "Needs Revision": +3 pts
  • Task rated "Meets Expectation": +5 pts
  • Task rated "Exceeds Expectation": +8 pts
  • 85% weekly task completion: +20 pts (auto-calculated every Sunday)
  • Presence streak: +10 pts/week
  • Task reporting streak: +10 pts/week

⚔ Point Deduction Criteria

  • Deductions logged by coordinator/admin for missed deadlines, poor conduct, or attendance violations.
  • Categories: attendance / task / learning / content / streak / conduct.

⚔ Streak Bonuses

  • Presence streak resets to zero if a member misses a single day in the week.
  • Task reporting streak is calculated independently from presence streak.

⚔ Rewards

  • Hero of the Week: highest points that week, confirmed by admin.
  • Hero of the Month: 300+ pts threshold, confirmed by admin, shown with SS-rank glow.

⚔ Violations & Consequences

  • Members below 100 pts are automatically flagged in the admin dashboard.
  • 3rd warning auto-triggers suspension status.
  • Violations reset monthly if compliant.

⚔ Dress Code

  • As specified by lab policy — enforced at coordinator/admin discretion.
