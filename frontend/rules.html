SYSTEM RULES

The rules that govern Vitals&Vectors. Violations have consequences.

1. WORKING HOURS & SCHEDULE
  • Official working hours are 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
  • Break time: 01:00 PM – 01:30 PM.
  • Coordinate with your team before taking extended breaks.
  • Unplanned absence during working hours may lead to deductions.
2. REPORTING & ATTENDANCE
  • Daily check-in is mandatory before 11:30 AM.
  • Late check-in after 11:30 AM without using a flex day will be marked as LATE.
  • Three absences in a week without prior notice will result in deductions.
  • Attendance is auto-logged through the V&V system.
3. TASK MANAGEMENT
  • All assigned tasks must be acknowledged within 2 hours.
  • Submit tasks before the deadline. Late submissions attract deductions.
  • If you face any blockers, inform your coordinator immediately.
  • Quality matters: incomplete or poor quality work will not be accepted.
4. POINT EARNING
  • Earn points by completing tasks, attending sessions, and contributing.
  • Bonus points for early submissions and exceptional performance.
  • Helping teammates and sharing resources earns contribution points.
  • Consistent performance leads to streak and milestone bonuses.
5. POINT DEDUCTIONS
  • Late arrivals, early exits, and unplanned leaves will lead to deductions.
  • Missing deadlines or submitting poor quality work will be penalized.
  • Negative behavior or policy violations attract heavy deductions.
  • Deductions are immediate and non-negotiable.
6. STREAK BONUSES
  • Maintain daily attendance streaks to earn bonus points.
  • Weekly streak (5 days): +20 pts  |  Bi-weekly (10 days): +50 pts
  • Monthly perfect attendance: +100 pts
  • Streaks reset on any absence without approved leave.
7. REWARDS
  • Top performers will be featured on the Leaderboard.
  • Monthly top performers receive special roles & perks.
  • Consistent contributors may unlock exclusive opportunities.
  • Rewards are performance-based and subject to change.
8. VIOLATIONS & CONSEQUENCES
  • Any misuse of the system or unfair practices will lead to strict action.
  • Repeated violations can result in suspension or removal.
  • Appeals can be made to the admin with valid justification.
  • All decisions by the admin panel are final.
9. DRESS CODE
  • Wear neat, clean and appropriate attire.
  • Avoid offensive prints, slogans or inappropriate clothing.
  • Follow a professional look during all official sessions.
  • Dress code violations may lead to deductions.
